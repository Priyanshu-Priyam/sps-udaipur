🏰 UDAIPUR ADVENTURE! 🏰
📅 Starting December 13th | Your Epic Journey Begins!
⭐ DAY 1: LAKES & PALACES EXTRAVAGANZA ⭐
🌅 MORNING
🏞️🛶
Gangaur & Ambrai Ghat
Start your day at the sacred ghats of Lake Pichola! Watch the sun rise over the water, see locals taking holy dips, and capture the stunning City Palace reflections. Ambrai Ghat offers the BEST photo ops with panoramic palace views! 📸✨

Pro Tip: Visit Bagore Ki Haveli museum nearby!
☀️ AFTERNOON
👑🏛️
City Palace - Royal Wonderland
Prepare to be AMAZED! This 450-year-old palace is Rajasthan's largest palace complex! Explore the:
🦚 Peacock Courtyard - stunning glass mosaics
✨ Mirror Palace - jaw-dropping beauty
🎨 Royal Museum - paintings & artifacts

Time needed: 2-3 hours of pure royal bliss!
🌆 EVENING
🌊🌅
Fateh Sagar Lake Sunset
End your day at the "Second Kashmir" of India! Take a boat ride to the three islands (Nehru Park has a zoo!), watch the magical sunset over the Aravalli Hills, and enjoy street food at the embankment. The water-jet fountain lights up at night! 💦✨
⭐ DAY 2: MOUNTAINS & NATURE CALLING ⭐
🌄 MORNING
🏰⛰️
Monsoon Palace (Raita/Sajjangarh Fort)
Climb 3,100 feet to this white marble wonder! Built in 1884 to watch monsoon clouds, it now offers 360° views of ALL of Udaipur! Fun fact: This was in the James Bond movie "Octopussy"! 🎬

Wildlife Bonus: Spot tigers, deer, and exotic birds in the surrounding sanctuary! 🐅🦌
🏔️ AFTERNOON
🥾⛰️
Bahubali Hills Trek
Adventure time! Hike the Instagram-famous Bahubali Hills! Only 15-20 minutes of easy climbing (1,500 steps) leads to breathtaking panoramas of Badi Lake and the Aravalli ranges!

📷 Perfect for: Sunset photos, nature vibes, and peaceful moments away from the city!
🍵 Tea and Maggi available at the top!
💡 Local's Secret: Visit Bahubali Hills early morning on a weekday to avoid crowds and get the BEST sunrise shots! The golden hour here is absolutely magical! ✨
